# v7.4 next-level premarket selector

## Why this rebuild exists

Recent reviews showed that the selector was still too willing to turn noisy premarket signals into BUY candidates.  The main failure modes were:

1. **Price-cost leakage**: `latest_change_pct` can reflect real-time or close change, while BUY decisions need the 09:25 auction change.  v7.4 makes `auction_change_pct` the first price-cost source.
2. **Theme overreach**: `THEME_CATCHUP` can produce big winners, but also produced large losers.  v7.4 makes theme-only signals watch-only before intraday confirmation.
3. **Cold/warming high-volatility traps**: STAR/ChiNext-style momentum in cold or cold-to-warming regimes needs exceptional amount, top rank, and multi-source confirmation.

## Production BUY sources

v7.4 only allows BUY from three durable premarket patterns:

- `LOW_OPEN_NET_REVERSAL`: discounted auction cost with real net/sustained order support.
- `CONFIRMED_MOMENTUM`: moderate auction gain, strong amount/liquidity, and independent order confirmation.
- `AUCTION_FOLLOW_THROUGH`: healthy-cost follow-through with multi-source evidence.

Everything else becomes `WATCH`, `REJECT`, or `AVOID`.

## Important rule changes

- `THEME_CATCHUP` is **not** a premarket BUY source anymore.
- Cold or cold-to-warming volatile-board momentum requires:
  - multi-source confirmation,
  - top best rank,
  - large auction amount.
- Cost is capped more aggressively: high auction cost is not allowed to become BUY.
- BUY count is regime-aware:
  - cold: at most 1,
  - cold-to-warming / warming: at most 2,
  - normal: at most 3.

## Review discipline

Close and excess return remain review-only.  They can diagnose missed winners and false positives, but they must not be used in production classification.
